My story

It started as a weekend hobby and became the work I love most.

Five years ago I began helping a friend edit his YouTube uploads. I fell in love with storytelling through design, logo work and color grading, and turned it into a full-time career.

What makes me different: I don't just put clips together. I build a narrative that keeps the viewer watching until the very end.

My mission

To help creators and brands turn raw, unorganized footage into polished stories that grow their audience.
